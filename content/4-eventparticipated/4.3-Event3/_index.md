# Event Review: "FCAJ - AGENTIC AI BUILD WEEK"
### Event Introduction
- Overview: The Agentic AI Buildweek (AAQ/AIBW) event was co-organized in Ho Chi Minh City. Designed as a hands-on Hackathon, it created a practical playground connecting developers to build AI agents and real-world applications.

- Opening Keynote Messages:

    + Technological Generational Shift: Previous generations took years to innovate (moving from 1 release/quarter to once every 2 weeks), but today's AI agents era allows continuous releases minute by minute.
    + Innovative Mindset: Encouraging developers to constantly challenge legacy mindsets without being constrained by outdated technical biases.
    + Human-in-the-Loop: No matter how large-scale technology or AI operates, technology remains just a tool. Humans are the ultimate decision-makers who shape the innovation cycle.
    + Lifelong Learning: Sustainable success comes from maintaining a spirit of continuous learning every day.

### Detailed Survey of Workshop Topics
### Team 1: AI Powered Conversational Ordering
- Problem & Solution:
    + Problem: Previous automated ordering AI experiments were prone to context loss (hallucination), leading to order errors. Additionally, current online ordering experiences are fragmented because users are forced to switch apps, create new accounts, and navigate complex menus.
    + Solution: Building an Omni-channel Chatbot Agent (focusing on popular messaging platforms like Zalo and WhatsApp) to order directly within the chat window. No app switching, no new account creation, maintaining a natural conversation flow.
- Lessons & Key Takeaways: Designing a strict order confirmation flow is key to handling LLM hallucinations when processing transactional data.

### Team 2: Multi-Agent Corporate Intelligence & Strategy Analytics
- Problem & Solution:
    + Problem: Strategic analysis data, financial reports, and competitor operational structures are often scattered, requiring significant time to collect and consolidate manually.
    + Solution: Gathering fragmented metrics and data from multiple sources, automatically consolidating and visualizing strategic structures. AI assists in impact analysis and ROI calculation.
- Lessons & Key Takeaways: Execution Over Idea: Theoretical concepts must be proven with a live Demo/POC. Selecting the right focused services helps solve the exact pain points of the problem.

### Team 3: SA Professional AI-Native App
- Problem & Solution:
    + Problem: Designing Cloud architecture diagrams, calculating costs, and writing Infrastructure as Code (IaC) manually under tight deadlines is labor-intensive and error-prone.
    + Solution: Analyzing natural language requirements -> Automatically generating visual architecture diagrams -> Supporting customization -> Exporting cost estimation tables and generating Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) -> Supporting automated deployment to AWS.
- Lessons & Key Takeaways: The core technical factor of an AI application lies in Prompt & Context Engineering, transforming ideas into an effective work assistant.
### Team 4: Computer Vision & AI Crowd Management System
- Problem & Solution:
    + Problem: Congestion in public areas (airports, events, checkpoints) negatively impacts operational flow and user experience.
    + Solution: Connecting CCTV surveillance systems to automatically detect density, analyze congested zones, and suggest timely staff dispatch plans.
- Lessons & Key Takeaways:
    + Resource Optimization: Optimizing lighter AI models significantly saves cloud infrastructure costs.
    + Scope Control: Limiting the feature set to a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) ensures delivery deadlines are met.

### Team 5: Adaptive Workflow Engine for AML
- Problem & Solution:
    + Problem: False positive rates in financial transaction monitoring are extremely high (90% - 95%). Manual checks consume significant time and resources from specialists.
    + Solution: Building a Multi-Agent system to automate information investigation, cash flow analysis, and evidence synthesis, reducing case processing time to a few minutes.
- Lessons & Key Takeaways: In sensitive domains, AI acts as a Co-pilot to augment human capabilities, combined with data transparency and traceability mechanisms.


### Key Knowledge & Technical Insights
### Transition from Single-Prompt to Multi-Agent Architecture
- Limitations of Single LLM Models: Relying on a single prompt to handle complex workflows (such as financial analysis or infrastructure automation) easily causes context overload and hallucinated outputs.
- Multi-Agent Architecture: Breaking large problems into specialized Agents handling distinct tasks (data ingestion, analysis, synthesis, error checking). This isolates context scope, increases accuracy, and simplifies component testing.
- Prompt & Context Engineering as the Backbone of AI-Native Apps
Context accounts for 80% of AI output quality. System design extends beyond model selection to encompass memory management, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and structured outputs (JSON/YAML) for seamless integration with traditional backend systems.

### Human-in-the-Loop & Traceability in High-Stakes Domains
- In high-risk operations (financial transactions, AML, payments), AI does not replace humans; it acts as an augmented Co-pilot.
- Agent systems must enforce strict traceability: every recommendation or action proposed by the AI must include evidence, reasoning paths, and clear citations for human verification before execution.

### Personal Observations & Takeaways
- Practical Workshop Experiences: 
    + Observing teams build and demo functional products within the short timeframe of a Hackathon provided valuable insights:
    + Focus on Real Pain Points: The most successful solutions were not necessarily those using the most complex tech, but those thoroughly resolving specific friction points (e.g., in-chat ordering or reducing AML verification time).
    + Execution Over Idea: The key differentiator lies in executing ideas into stable Demo/POC builds with proper error handling, rather than relying on theoretical slides.

### Connection to Internship & Career Roadmap
- The event helped align technical focus for upcoming professional milestones:
    + Full-stack & Cloud Optimization: Understanding how to integrate AI models into existing backend services and automate infrastructure (IaC/AWS) provides a strong advantage in real-world projects.
    + Problem-Solving Mindset: A engineer's core value extends beyond writing clean code to leveraging AI tools for optimizing workflows and driving user value.

### Community Value & Networking
- The event served as a bridge to learn practical industry experiences from mentors/experts and connect with passionate developers.

- The open discussion and constructive feedback atmosphere inspired confidence in the rapid growth of the local AI engineering community.

### Summary of Core Lessons
- Human-in-the-Loop: Technology and AI are tools. Human critical thinking, domain insight, and final decisions determine product value.

- Lean Thinking (MVP): Always start with the smallest working version, gather feedback, and iterate rapidly rather than building overly complex systems upfront.

- Lifelong Learning: Technology evolves weekly; proactively adopting new approaches and discarding outdated methods is essential to stay ahead.
